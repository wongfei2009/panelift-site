PaneLift for Mac

Free. It runs in your menu bar and shares your windows with PaneLift on your iPhone and iPad.

For Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS 14 or later. Notarized by Apple.

PaneLift for iPhone and iPad

Free, coming soon to the App Store. Needs iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

Install

  1. Open PaneLift.dmg and drag PaneLift to Applications.
  2. Open PaneLift. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.
  3. Allow Screen Recording (to show your windows) and Accessibility (to click and type from your device). After Screen Recording, choose Quit & Reopen.

Update

PaneLift tells you in its menu when a new version is out. Download it here and replace the copy in Applications; your settings and paired devices stay.

Uninstall

Remove your devices in PaneLift’s Settings › Devices, quit PaneLift and move it to the Trash. The privacy policy lists what it leaves behind.
