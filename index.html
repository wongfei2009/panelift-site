PaneLift

Any window from your Mac, on your iPhone and iPad.

Download PaneLift for Mac

Free for Mac. The iPhone and iPad app is coming soon to the App Store.

The whole screen is your Mac

No toolbars or panels. Every control sits in one small pill you can drag anywhere, or fold out of the way.

A desktop shaped like your device

Your Mac’s Desktop appears on a display sized to your iPhone or iPad, so it fills the screen with no black bars.

Any window, sharp and live

Pick a window from Switch and it fills your screen. It keeps updating even when it’s covered on your Mac.

Keys where you type

Esc, Tab, Control, Command and the arrows sit on top of the keyboard, and typing follows your Mac’s layout, so terminals get the right keys. Or hold the mic and speak: let go, and the words are typed on your Mac.

Touch or trackpad

Tap to click and drag to scroll, or turn the whole screen into a trackpad that works like a Mac’s: two fingers scroll, swipe between pages, pinch and rotate, and three fingers drag.

Private and direct

Over Wi-Fi, a USB cable, or your own VPN away from home. No account, no server.

Get started

  1. Download PaneLift for Mac and open it. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when it asks.
  2. Get PaneLift on your iPhone or iPad (coming soon to the App Store).
  3. Tap your Mac and scan the code it shows. Over a USB cable, they pair by themselves.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 14 or later, and an iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

Built on Sill

PaneLift starts from Sill, Noah Saffer’s open-source app, and redesigns it for the small screen: one pill instead of toolbars, a desktop in your device’s shape, keys on the keyboard, typing that follows your Mac’s layout, and simpler settings on both sides.
