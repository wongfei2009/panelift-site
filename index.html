PaneLift

Any window from your Mac, on your iPhone and iPad.

Download PaneLift for Mac

PaneLift for Mac is free; PaneLift for iPhone and iPad is a paid app, coming soon to the App Store. PaneLift for Mac needs Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later; the app needs iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

PaneLift shows a window from your Mac, or its whole desktop, on your iPhone or iPad, and lets you use it with touch, a keyboard or Apple Pencil. It streams straight from your Mac to your device over your own network, so there is no account to create and no server in between. You pair each device once, and every connection is encrypted. PaneLift for Mac is free, and the PaneLift app for iPhone and iPad is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no subscription.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon, on macOS 14 or later. When PaneLift first opens, you allow it Screen Recording and Accessibility.
  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, on the same network as your Mac.
  • Away from home, your own VPN, such as Tailscale, with Remote Access turned on in PaneLift.

No shared network? Connect your iPhone or iPad to your Mac with a USB cable, or turn on Direct Wireless Connection in PaneLift on your Mac.

On iPhone Duo, PaneLift follows the fold: open flat on its side, your Mac fills the inner display; half open like a laptop, the picture is above the fold with a trackpad and keys below; closed, PaneLift runs on the cover display.

Connect in four steps

  1. Download PaneLift for Mac, drag it to your Applications folder and open it. PaneLift lives in the menu bar.
  2. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when PaneLift asks. When macOS offers Quit & Reopen, choose it.
  3. Get PaneLift for iPhone and iPad from the App Store (coming soon). Open it and allow Local Network.
  4. Tap your Mac, and pair once: it shows a code for your iPhone or iPad to scan, and over a USB cable the two pair by themselves. Then its desktop appears, and you can pick any window from Switch.

Away from home

With Remote Access on, PaneLift reaches your Mac through your own VPN, such as Tailscale, or through a port you forward on your router. An iPhone or iPad you paired learns how the next time it connects to your Mac at home with Remote Access on; for one that never connected at home, choose Pair iPhone or iPad… in the PaneLift menu on your Mac, and tap Add a Mac… on the device. Away from home, PaneLift starts at a quality a slower connection can carry, and when you get home, the session moves to your network by itself.

Private by design

PaneLift has no ads, no tracking and no account, and it never sends anything to its developer. The privacy policy has the details.
